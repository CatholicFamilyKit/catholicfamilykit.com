What ages is Catholic Family Kit designed for?+

Designed for families with children ages 7–11. Chelsea builds each activity to work across this range — a 7-year-old and an 11-year-old can do the same activity at different depths. The Activity Guide includes discussion questions tiered by age. Many families tell us their 12–15 year olds engage equally well, especially in discussion.

When does the kit ship? When will it arrive?+

We ship between the 25th–27th of each month via USPS Priority Mail so kits arrive by the 1st of the following month — giving you all month to use it in rhythm. You're billed today for your first kit, then on the 1st of each month after; the 24th is our monthly order cutoff, not your billing date. Subscribe on or before the 24th and your first kit ships this cycle (arriving by the 1st). Subscribe on the 25th or later — once that cycle's kits have already shipped — and your first kit is the following month's. All plans include free US shipping.

Can I cancel? Are there contracts?+

No contracts, ever. You're billed on the 1st of each month, so you can cancel anytime before the 1st and you won't be charged again — your subscription simply ends. If you've already been billed on the 1st for the upcoming kit, that kit will still ship to you (it's yours), and no further charges follow. You can also skip individual months instead of canceling — just skip before the 1st. Promotional pricing stays locked for as long as you maintain your subscription. You can cancel or manage your subscription online anytime.

How do I cancel?+

The quickest way is to manage or cancel your subscription online through our secure billing portal. You can also visit our contact page or email hello@catholicfamilykit.com with your name, address, and email requesting to cancel and we will be sure to do it that day!

Is the content doctrinally accurate? How do I know?+

Every kit is reviewed by a credentialed seminary professor of theology before it ships. Every activity guide, saint card, prayer, and educational booklet is checked for fidelity to the Magisterium and the Catechism of the Catholic Church. No other Catholic subscription kit offers this level of theological oversight.

Do you offer one-time purchases (no subscription)?+

At this time, we focus on the subscription model so we can keep production tightly tied to the liturgical calendar and seminary review. If you'd like a single kit as a trial, contact us and we'll send the current month's kit at the regular monthly rate, no commitment.

Can I send a kit as a gift?+

Absolutely — our boxes make wonderful gifts for godchildren, nieces and nephews, grandchildren, and fellow Catholic families. Enter the recipient's shipping address at checkout. You can gift individual boxes or purchase a prepaid annual subscription. The physicality of the kit is intentional — it's part of the formation experience.

What happens to my price when the promotion ends?+

Nothing — the rate you subscribe at is the rate you keep for as long as your subscription stays active and continuous. Subscribe during the current promotion (which ends August 24 at midnight CT) and you keep that promotional rate on every renewal. After the promotion, new subscriptions are $29.99/mo (Monthly), $84.99/qtr (Quarterly), or $299.99/yr (Annual). If you cancel and re-subscribe later, the then-current rate applies.

How long does an activity take?+

Most activities run 25–40 minutes including the discussion. Designed to fit between dinner and bedtime. The guide tells you exactly how long each piece takes so you can plan accordingly.

Do you ship internationally?+

Currently we ship to the United States with free shipping. International shipping (Canada, UK, Latin America, Ireland) is on our roadmap. Email us to be added to the international waitlist so you know when CFK arrives in your country!

My family is Eastern Catholic / Byzantine. Will this fit?+

The kit is designed around the Roman Rite liturgical calendar. Many Eastern Catholic families still find it valuable since the saints, virtues, and core catechesis are universal. We're working on Eastern Rite supplemental materials — contact us to be notified.

My kit arrived damaged. What do I do?+

Email hello@catholicfamilykit.com within 7 days of delivery with a photo. We ship a replacement within 5 business days at no charge — full stop.

1. Free Shipping

Every Catholic Family Kit subscription includes free standard shipping to any address in the United States. There are no shipping fees, handling fees, or hidden charges. The price you see is the price you pay.

2. Ship Window & Delivery Timeline

Kits ship between the 25th and 27th of each month via USPS Priority Mail. They are designed to arrive by the 1st of the following month, giving you a full liturgical month to enjoy your kit from the moment it arrives. USPS Priority Mail typically delivers in 2–5 business days.

You will receive a shipping confirmation email with your tracking number when your kit ships. Catholic Family Kit LLC is not responsible for carrier delays once the package is in USPS possession.

3. Subscription Cutoff

Subscribe on or before the 24th: you receive that cycle's physical kit, shipped between the 25th–27th and arriving by the 1st.

Subscribe on the 25th or later: that cycle's kits have already shipped, so your first physical kit will be the following cycle's kit.

This cutoff is separate from your billing date (the 1st). It is applied consistently and is determined by the timestamp on your subscription. There are no exceptions — we apply the rule uniformly so every family is treated fairly. Kits are also subject to a monthly inventory limit; once a cycle sells out, new orders roll to the next cycle.

4. Hawaii, Alaska & US Territories

We ship to Hawaii, Alaska, Puerto Rico, and US territories via USPS Priority Mail at no extra charge. Free shipping applies to every US address — there is no surcharge.

5. International Shipping

We do not currently ship internationally. Email hello@catholicfamilykit.com to be added to our international waitlist and we will notify you when international shipping becomes available.

6. Damaged Kits

If your kit arrives damaged, email hello@catholicfamilykit.com within 7 days of the delivery date with a photograph of the damage. We will ship a replacement kit at no charge within 5 business days. We reserve the right to request the damaged kit be returned before issuing a replacement at our discretion.

7. Lost or Missing Kits

If tracking shows "Delivered" but you have not received your kit, please first check with neighbors, building management, and all household members, and allow 48 hours from the delivery scan. If the kit remains missing after 48 hours, email hello@catholicfamilykit.com. We will file a USPS claim and ship a replacement if the loss is confirmed.

Catholic Family Kit LLC is not liable for packages confirmed delivered by USPS to the address on file.

8. Address Changes

To update your shipping address, email hello@catholicfamilykit.com or update it in your account portal on or before the 24th of the month. Address updates received after the 24th take effect the following month's shipment. We cannot redirect packages already in transit. Catholic Family Kit LLC is not responsible for shipments delivered to an outdated address that was not updated by the 24th deadline.

9. Returns

Due to the perishable and personalized nature of our monthly kits, we do not accept returns of shipped and delivered kits. If you believe you received the wrong kit or a kit with missing items, contact us within 7 days of delivery and we will make it right.

10. Cancellations

You're billed on the 1st of each month. To stop your next charge, log into your billing portal or email hello@catholicfamilykit.com before the 1st. If you cancel after you've been billed on the 1st, that kit has already been paid for and will ship to you as normal; your subscription then ends with no further charges. You will not receive a refund for a kit that has already been charged, assembled, or shipped. Annual subscribers who cancel mid-year are not entitled to a pro-rata refund after the kit for that month has shipped.

11. Limitation of Liability

Catholic Family Kit's liability for any shipping-related claim is limited to the value of the subscription kit for that month. Catholic Family Kit LLC is not liable for indirect, consequential, or incidental damages arising from shipping delays, carrier errors, or address discrepancies provided by the subscriber. By subscribing, you agree to these terms.

Returns, Refunds & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: July 2026

If you've already been billed for the upcoming kit, that one kit still ships to you, then your subscription ends. Monthly subscription payments are non-refundable once the payment has been processed on your billing date (the 1st of each month, or the day you first subscribe for your first kit). Annual subscription payments are non-refundable after the first kit for the year has shipped. Annual subscribers who cancel mid-year are not entitled to a pro-rata refund after any individual month's kit has shipped.

4. Promotional Rates — Important Notice

Subscribers who cancel permanently forfeit any promotional locked rate. Re-subscribing after cancellation is at the then-current regular rate. Promotional rates are non-transferable and apply only to the original, continuously-active account.

5. How to Cancel

The fastest way is to manage or cancel your subscription online through our secure billing portal — no phone call required. You can also email hello@catholicfamilykit.com with "Cancel Subscription" in the subject line, including the email address on your account. You may also call (832) 525-2593 Monday–Friday, 8am–4pm CT. We process cancellations within one business day and send written confirmation.

6. Damaged or Wrong Kits

If your kit arrives damaged, defective, or contains the wrong items, email us within 7 days of delivery with a photograph. We ship a replacement at no charge within 5 business days. Full details in our Shipping Policy.

Need help? Contact us or call (832) 525-2593. We're a small Catholic family business and we're here to make it right.
